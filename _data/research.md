## Ongoing Research

### Security and Privacy of AI-Driven Systems

Modern digital systems increasingly rely on AI-driven decision-making and large-scale data collection. My research interests focus on understanding the **security, privacy, and trust challenges** that emerge in such systems, particularly in real-world environments involving third-party platforms and adversarial behavior.

I am especially interested in:
- Measuring and modeling adversarial activity in online ecosystems  
- Privacy risks arising from data aggregation and opaque data flows  
- The use of machine learning for security analysis and prediction  
- Human factors and usability in security and privacy mechanisms  

**Methods:** machine learning, system measurement, network and traffic analysis, statistical analysis, qualitative analysis.
